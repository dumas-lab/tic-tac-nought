Privacy

Pre-alpha 0.3.3 · Last updated 25 October 2026

Tic Tac Nought is a small pre-alpha game. This page explains what the online parts store and why. Local play works without an account and without contacting the game server.

What stays on your device

The game saves run progress, the local damage ledger, and sound settings in your browser or desktop app storage. These stay on your device unless you create an account and choose to upload a save. Guests never sync saves.

Accounts

Accounts use a handle and password, with no email address. We store the handle, a password hash managed by our authentication provider, your best Endless score and run details, submission timestamps, account status, and — only if you use save sync — the save payload you upload. The handle and best score are shown publicly on the global board.

Guests

If you submit a score without creating an account, we create an anonymous guest identity with an auto-generated name such as Guest-7F3K2Q. We store that identity, the generated name, your best score and run details, and submission timestamps. Guest scores appear on the global board with a GUEST badge. Guest saves are never uploaded.

Global leaderboard

The board is public. It shows a handle or guest name, guest status, score, round, board size, level, and rank. It does not show your password, email (we do not collect one), device ID, or save contents.

Rate limiting and abuse prevention

Account creation and guest claiming are rate-limited using a salted hash of your IP address. The raw address is not stored, and these records are deleted after seven days. Scores and saves may be reviewed or removed to keep the pre-alpha board usable. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Who processes data

  • GitHub Pages serves the web/PWA build. GitHub receives standard request logs such as IP address and user agent under its own privacy policy.
  • Supabase hosts authentication, the database, and the small server functions. The projects are hosted in the EU (eu-central-1).

Retention and deletion

Account data remains until you delete the account. You can delete it from the in-game Account panel; this removes the account, its global score, and its cloud save. The local save on your device is not deleted. Guest identities can also be deleted from the Account panel. Rate-limit hashes expire after seven days.

Your choices

You can play entirely offline and never create an account or submit a score. You can sign out, delete an account, and export or import your local save from Options. Because accounts have no email address, password resets are handled manually by the maintainer; open an issue at github.com/dumas-lab/tic-tac-nought/issues if you need help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

This is a pre-alpha project, so this policy may change as the online features change. The updated date at the top will change when it does.

Questions: open an issue.
